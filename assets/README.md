# Assets

Original artwork and product visuals for Sentinel Forge.

Everything placed here must be original work owned by Talal Al Ghafri, or
licensed material whose licence is recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`. No
asset may be copied or derived from another product's branding or interface.

Blender may be used to produce logos, product artwork and documentation visuals.
It must never become a runtime dependency: rendered output is committed here,
not generated at build or run time.

Empty in this build. Promotional material is deliberately deferred until the
engineering core is proven.
